# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, as specified in the project brief.

## Users

Recruiters and hiring teams reviewing Nabil Pradipta Setiawan's profile while considering candidates for professional opportunities.

## Product Purpose

A single-page professional portfolio that presents Nabil's background, education, skills, work and organization experience, and public email contact. Success means a recruiter can understand his profile quickly and reach him by email.

## Positioning

An evidence-led profile of an Industrial Engineering graduate whose documented experience spans tapioca flour production operations and used-car marketing and sales, with stated interests in supply chain, production planning, procurement, and data analysis.

## Operating Context

Visitors scan the page on desktop or mobile while evaluating a job candidate. The site is in Indonesian and should support job applications and personal branding.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page navigation with responsive menu, section links, email link, and a CV download only when the real PDF is available.
- No backend, database, or contact form.
- Do not publish the WhatsApp number, home address, unverified LinkedIn/GitHub links, or Instagram unless publication is approved.
- Do not invent work history, organizations, project work, certificates, awards, performance metrics, or skill levels.
- Keep Supply Chain Management, Production Planning, and Procurement framed as interests, not professional expertise.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Nabil Pradipta Setiawan. The interface is Indonesian, light, professional, restrained, and burgundy-led, using the palette and direction in the supplied brief. Voice should be natural and appropriate for a fresh graduate.

## Evidence on Hand

The supplied brief contains the approved profile copy, education, experience, skills, language levels, and public email. The project folder currently contains no CV PDF or photographs; those assets must not be fabricated or replaced with assumed identities.

## Product Principles

- Put verifiable candidate information before decoration.
- Make the experience and education easy to scan.
- Keep interests distinct from proven skills.
- Publish only approved contact information.
- Keep the site usable without a build system or backend.
